Our Mission: Empowering Charities in India

SOS Child Charitable Trust works every day to bring food, education, shelter, medicine and dignity to those who need it most. From sponsoring a child's schooling and delivering medicines, to caring for our elders and responding to disasters, our volunteers and donors make real change happen across India.

Shelter Home

Our Shelter Home

Thousands of children in India grow up without parents or a safe place to call home. Our shelter home gives them clothing, nutritious food, a warm home, education and the joy of celebrating festivals together like a family.

Your support helps every child grow up with love, safety and the confidence to build a better future.

Education

Education for Every Child

Education is the surest path out of poverty. We run regular drives to provide stationery, school bags, uniforms, raincoats and fees so that no child has to leave school for lack of resources.

Sponsor a child's education today and open the door to a brighter tomorrow.

Old Age

Our Old Age Home

Many elders are abandoned in their final years. We care for them with food, medicines, companionship and respect. With rising costs, every contribution matters.

Donate, volunteer or simply visit and spend time with our elders.
